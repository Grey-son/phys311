The Block 2 Checkpoint demo showcases my engine in two simulations.

Simulation 1 simulates a hot and cold gas as red and blue particles initialized in two separate boxes connected
by a thin tube. The simulation represents how pressure equalizes, with more hot (red) particles moving over to
the cold (blue) side, than vice versa. Initial and final state pictures are provided in the images file. This
simulation showcases the versatility of the wall object and effectiveness of broad phase optimization for 
collisions.

Simulation 2 showcases the engine's spring and coulomb force classes, with four pairs of spring coupled bodies
initialized in a box with various charges. For this simulation, energy is not very well conserved. I show in a 
plot that energy conservation is valid for small values of dt. Both the plot and initial/final state images 
simulation 2 can be found in the images file.
